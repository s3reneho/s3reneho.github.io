Political Economy · Comparative & International

Serene Ho

Ph.D. Candidate · MIT Political Science

I study how heterogeneity among firms reshapes political behavior and the institutions that govern labor markets and trade. My dissertation, Heterogeneous Firms and the Politics of Institutional Change, bridges comparative political economy and international political economy: it takes CPE's attention to institutions and IPE's attention to firms' global position, and asks what happens when firms genuinely differ from one another.

Working with confidential U.S. Census microdata (LEHD, LFTTD, LBD) and firm-level trade records, I trace how differences in productivity and export exposure split firms' political interests — and, in turn, remake the institutions meant to regulate them.
